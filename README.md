This is a workspace for the very first edition of the Geohabari Studio Challenge
The goal of this group challenge is to address the flooding problem with the study area being Nairobi. 
This is mean;t to be a learning project taking our community through the thought process of solving spatial or geographic problems right from the start to having a working solution. 
Key stakeholders are early career professionals and techies who are curious on the role of maps and spatial datasets in addressing pressing societal challenges.

* **📂 `database/`** – Managed by the **Database Team**. Contains schemas, ETL scripts and migrations.
* **📂 `analysis_modeling/`** – Managed by the **Analysis & Modeling Team**. Contains Jupyter notebooks, Python/R scripts, and model artifacts.
* **📂 `data_viz/`** – Managed by the **Data Viz Team**. Contains dashboard source files, Streamlit apps, and asset layouts.
